Gabriele's mentor, a priest from Earth kidnapped in Althon's Gate and sent to Seranthis

Role
Catholic priest; Gabriele's mentor and spiritual father
Race
Human

he/him

soft-spoken, deliberate; guides with questions; sits in silence when words do not help

Now

  • Missing. Seized at the poor-quarter mission by Seranthis-accented men about two weeks before S1, then handed to a Seranthis-bound caravan at the Old Tallow Yard. S4
  • Ilna Vesk traded him to Dreeshen Vale's people for funding and equipment. Where in Seranthis he went is unknown. S5
  • Gabriele wears his rosary and studies his annotated prayer book, whose notes match real spells. S3

Who they are

  • Sent by the Pope to investigate trouble on Crete; when he went silent, the Pope sent Gabriele after him. S1
  • 76. A lifetime serving the poor and the Church; no fighter. prep
  • Found Gabriele drunk on his church steps, took him in, and later offered him the priesthood. prep
  • Spent about three months among the poor of Althon's Gate before he was taken; beloved there. prep
  • Needs: to be found, and told the truth about what he has enabled. He is starting to see the mage he trusted is not what she claimed. prep
  • Under stress: quieter. He prays, waits, and looks for what can still be done. prep
  • Catchphrase: "Only God can know everything; but it is our duty to learn all we can of the bounty he left us." prep
  • Three words: gentle, perceptive, unshakeable. prep
DM only

Pulled to Ossë on purpose by Dreeshen's summoning magic, for the Benefactor. The same magic pulled Gabriele through three months later.

His forbidden research reached across realms before he left: he tried to speak to God and reached the Devil.

Delivered to the Benefactor as Dreeshen's agreed payment for support of the uprising. What the Benefactor needs him for is still TBD in Notion.

A pure soul being slowly corrupted, not fully aware of it; that purity is what the Benefactor needs. (Tom's ruling, 2026-11-18)
