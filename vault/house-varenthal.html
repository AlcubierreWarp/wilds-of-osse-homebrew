House Varenthal

also The Crown, Varenthal Crown
Faction · governmentactive S1first seen S1●●●●●

Royal house of Varenthal under Queen Sabine; hosts the Royal Tournament that decides the crown

Leader
Sabine Varenthal

Now

  • Standing with the party: the Queen told Timbit "the crown is in your debt". S12
  • Royal Tournament postponed two months; live combat training banned; Crown rune reserves sealed. S11
  • Blames foreign enemies for the planted runes. S11
  • Pledged Crown money to rebuild the Kilnworks. S8

What it is

  • Royal house of Varenthal, ruling from Althon's Gate. S1
  • Seranthis split from Varenthal about 250 years ago. S3
  • Founded by exiles from Aglarond; chose partnership with the Oss'kai. prep
  • Hosts the Royal Tournament. S4
  • The tournament runs yearly; noble holdings renew on a five-year cycle; a crown year falls when the heir turns 18, and the heir must win outright. prep
  • Prince Elias Varenthal is the crown prince. S11
  • Goals: secure the throne for Elias; hold off Seranthis under King Draven II. prep
DM only

Factions within the Crown rig the tournament for Elias, and the Crown removes challengers; Cassian was the first case.

Sabine hired a killer (the Hand) for Cassian through Unbowed middlemen. The Unbowed widened the job to every champion on the Benefactor's behalf and chose her own toast as the signal, so the Crown wears it.

Lady Odile's promise to Elias was made through Sabine.
