S3 - The Glowing Goat

Session

Belaskro's flashback ties the Pyreborn mark to his family; Rosalia's tip leads to the Glowing Goat and a fight with Dask Corrin's Seranthis crew

What happened

  • Flashback, 32 years ago in Shadowvale: twelve-year-old Belaskro Tich and Sloane Tich watch Uncle Corvane Aerrowyn and Aunt Maribel Aerrowyn leave for Althon's Gate after Corvane wins a tournament and a title.
  • Maribel gives the siblings matching moon and sun rings; the mark on the wagon stands for the bond between the families. Maribel later died bearing Cassian Aerrowyn. Belaskro has never sought his uncle out.
  • Rosalia Whitlock saw four or five men with Seranthis accents drag Father Barichello toward the docks two weeks ago; one drinks at The Glowing Goat.
  • Malachai Wrent explains Kingdom of Seranthis: a rival kingdom from a split 250 years ago that took Oss'kai women as brides by force.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci confirms the rosary is Barichello's and wears it; the prayer book's annotations match real spells, including the toll the dead he cast in S2.
  • Wrent tells GUS privately to watch Gabriele; extends Belaskro's contract for two more gold; leaves to report.
  • Yorwick Dray sends GUS's steel defender to the docks.
  • At the Goat, the bartender tells the Sundermark Brides history; Belaskro overhears Dask Corrin, Ivo and a third sailor on a finished kidnapping, a caravan and pending payment, and hunter's-marks Dask.
  • GUS's literal orders get Gabriele cuffed and fined for public urination; Belaskro pays 15 sp.
  • The party tails the sailors; Dask spots GUS and turns on them in a side street. Combat begins.

What changed

  • New: Dask Corrin, Ivo, Yorwick Dray; Sloane Tich, Corvane Aerrowyn, Maribel Aerrowyn seen in flashback.
  • Revealed: the Pyreborn mark is the moon-and-sun crest of Corvane's and Maribel's union; Corvane Aerrowyn is Belaskro's uncle and Cassian Aerrowyn his cousin.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci wears Barichello's rosary (was in GUS's evidence compartment).
  • Thread advanced: Find Father Barichello (Seranthis kidnappers found).

Clues

  • ⚑ Seranthis men took Barichello toward the docks: landed.
  • ⚑ The kidnappers drink at the Glowing Goat: landed.
  • ⚑ Dreeshen Vale hired the abduction: not landed (session ended at the start of the fight).
  • ⚑ Caravan route toward Seranthis: partly landed (a caravan and payment overheard).
  • Crest on the wagon is the Aerrowyn bond: landed.

Plans

  • Flashback, Rosalia and Glowing Goat scenes all played; the planned interrogation turned into a street fight, so Dreeshen Vale's name was not reached.

Open going into S4

  • Mid-fight with Dask's crew in a dockside alley.
  • Who paid for Barichello's kidnapping, and where was he taken?
  • Why does Bram wear the crest from Corvane's wagon?
