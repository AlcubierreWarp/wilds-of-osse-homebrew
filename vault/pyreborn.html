Poor-Quarter gang under Bram Kell, marked by the sun-and-moon crest; a supply line in the rune sabotage

Leader
Bram Kell

Now

  • Standing with the party: enemies since the siege of the mission; the party raided their safehouse and took Nessa Ruhl. S9
  • Nessa Ruhl freed by brigands; "the plan" is still on. S11

What it is

  • Criminal gang of the Poor-Quarter, moderate power. prep
  • Mark: a crescent moon in a sun, the crest of Corvane and Maribel Aerrowyn's union. S3
  • Led by Bram Kell; hovel ten minutes east of the mission; outnumber the Unbowed ten to one. S5
  • Rhys believes a Varenthal noble house directs them, with ties to Seranthis. S4
  • Ran a hidden workshop under Vantry Runeworks forging sabotaged shield runes, worked by coerced labour whose families were held hostage. S7
  • A gold sun-and-moon signet marks high rank. S7
  • A dying worker said Belaskro looks just like their leader. S7
  • Nessa Ruhl's ledger records names, dates and payments under the crest. S10
DM only

Corvane Aerrowyn is "the boss"; Bram runs the gang day to day. The party is walking toward Corvane and Belaskro's own family.

The brand is the old Aerrowyn warrior-clan mark. The gang financed House Aerrowyn's rise through a black market in contraband wards.

Ilna Vesk's intel on them is self-interested.

Pell Marrow skimmed Vantry blanks for them; the Knives' escape tablets at the gala were cut from that stock (S12 plan, not landed).
