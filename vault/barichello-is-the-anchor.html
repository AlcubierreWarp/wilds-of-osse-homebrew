Barichello is the anchor

Secrethidden from the partyprep●●●●●

Father Barichello is the living anchor the dark mage needs for her ritual, and he opened the door himself

The truth

  • Barichello came to Ossë pursuing forbidden knowledge in service of God, and was corrupted and manipulated across dimensional lines for years. He reached the Devil when he reached for God. prep
  • He is the anchor of a ritual that opens permanent passage between Earth and Ossë. Somewhere between captive and compromised; beginning to understand what he enabled; capable of small resistance; not free. prep
  • Dreeshen's operatives took him about two weeks before S1 and delivered him to the Benefactor as payment. prep
  • Ruled: he is a pure soul being slowly corrupted, not fully aware of it; his faith still holds. The "secretly evil" reading is dropped. (Tom's ruling, 2026-11-18)
