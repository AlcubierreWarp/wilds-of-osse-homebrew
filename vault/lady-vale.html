DM only

She is Dreeshen Vale's mother. She paid Rosalia Whitlock, for her mixed Oss'kai heritage, to leave Seranthis, and never told Dreeshen where she went. (Tom's ruling, 2026-11-18) It cost her the son instead: Dreeshen walked away from his name to look for Rosalia.

She is oblivious. She does not know he founded the Unbowed or that he means to tear down the Seranthis noble system. She still expects him home.

The party will likely read her as proof of state sponsorship of the Unbowed. It is the opposite. Let the misread run.
