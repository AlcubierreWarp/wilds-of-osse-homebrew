S5 - Unholy Alliances

Session

Two lines converge on Ilna Vesk at the Fair Maiden: Barichello went to Dreeshen Vale's people in Seranthis, and Ilna turns informant against the Pyreborn

What happened

  • Malachai Wrent confirms the job is black money: if caught, the Arcane Marshals disavow them. He names The Unbowed; the party ties it to the raised-fist seal.
  • GUS and Belaskro Tich go to the Old Tallow Yard; GUS's homunculus finds Krill Nye hiding in the wagons. Krill agrees to lead them to his boss for his freedom.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci and Wrent interrogate Dask Corrin in The Sanctuary: Ilna Vesk leads the Unbowed in Varenthal; Dreeshen Vale ("DV") commands from Kingdom of Seranthis.
  • Gabriele stages a violent outburst so Wrent can "ruin" the case and release Dask; they tail him.
  • Both lines meet at The Fair Maiden: Belaskro sees through a drag act; the performer, "Lady Macbeth", is Ilna.
  • In her cellar office Ilna demands GUS leave; Belaskro refuses, calling GUS a person. Gabriele and Wrent burst in; Ilna fires at Wrent and misses; Gabriele's guiding bolt drops her.
  • Ilna: she sent Father Barichello to Dreeshen's people for money and equipment, and does not know where in Seranthis he went.
  • GUS's records: the Vale name ties to Lady Vale of the Seranthis King's Council.
  • Ilna scorns the Pyreborn and denies any part in tournament sabotage. Their leader is Bram Kell; their hovel is ten minutes east of the mission; they outnumber the Unbowed ten to one.
  • Wrent lets Ilna go to keep the job off the books; his Pyreborn raid requests are always denied by the King's Council.
  • Gabriele heals Dask, gives him a gold coin, and calls it the start of a relationship.

What changed

  • New: Ilna Vesk met, The Fair Maiden; Dreeshen Vale and Lady Vale named.
  • Ilna Vesk becomes a source against the Pyreborn, off the books.
  • Dask Corrin released from Marshal custody; Gabriele's informant.
  • Krill Nye caught at the yard, then freed.
  • Revealed: Dreeshen Vale is "DV" and directs the Unbowed from Seranthis.
  • Threads: Who is DV resolved; The deal with the Unbowed opened; Find Father Barichello now points to Dreeshen Vale in Seranthis.

Clues

  • ⚑ Barichello was payment, not a target: partly landed (traded for funding and equipment; the Benefactor behind Dreeshen not raised).
  • ⚑ The Unbowed name reaches the table: landed (Wrent).
  • ⚑ Ilna is worth more talking than jailed: landed (Wrent let her go).
  • ⚑ The road runs through Ilna to Dreeshen Vale and Seranthis: landed.
  • The second, inbound caravan with Dreeshen's fee: not landed.
  • Pyreborn hovel ten minutes east of the mission, led by Bram Kell: landed.
  • Rosalia's name in front of Ilna (sealed): not raised.

Plans

  • Wrent's briefing played. The yard turned on Krill, not the second caravan. Ilna was met at the Fair Maiden instead of beneath the Saltings, and the meeting ended in a short fight before it became talk.

Open going into S6

  • Where in Seranthis is Barichello?
  • Which Varenthal house shields the Pyreborn?
  • Take Bram Kell away from his gang, or go at the tournament as arena staff?
