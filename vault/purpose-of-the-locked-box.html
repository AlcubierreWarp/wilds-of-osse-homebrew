The question

  • What is the box Gabriele carried, who made it, and what is it for? S1

What the party knows

  • Gabriele d'Avidicci carried it for the Pope aboard the Santa Marie under sealed orders; he does not know what is inside. S1
  • It was recovered with him after the wreck. Marshal mages read enough power in it to level Althon's Gate. S1
  • A matching magical signature has been detected in Kingdom of Seranthis. S1
  • The Arcane Marshals hold it at The Sanctuary. S1
  • No progress since S1.

Clues

  • Catastrophic power reading. S1
  • Matching Seranthis signature, from Marshal intelligence. S1
  • Found near Gabriele, not on him. S1
DM only
  • It is an arcane anchor: a black stone glowing from its centre, supplied to Dreeshen Vale by the Dark Mage and activated by blood magic to pull Father Barichello across realms. The pull refracted and made a second anchor that pulled Gabriele. prep
  • Fra Alonso de Vergara hanged a crewman who tried to force it open. prep
