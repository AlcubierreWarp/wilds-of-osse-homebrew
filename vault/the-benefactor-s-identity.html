The Benefactor's identity

also The Benefactor, the Dark Mage, the Big Bad
Secrethidden from the partyprep●●●●●

The Unbowed's Benefactor is the Big Bad: a warped Oss'kai Mage Adept in the barrens who means to reach Earth

The truth

  • A former Oss'kai Mage Adept, caught in a Spellplague change field and fused with the shadow creatures she was sent to hunt, has been building power in the barrens at the edge of the known world. prep
  • She wants Earth, which has no magic and no defences. Ossë is the staging ground. prep
  • Her ritual needs a living dimensional anchor to open permanent passage: Father Barichello. prep See Barichello is the anchor.
  • She backs the Unbowed with magic and material. Dreeshen paid by delivering Barichello. He deals only through intermediaries and does not know who or what she is. prep
  • She touches temporal manipulation at the edges: the lure for Gabriele. prep
  • Open fork: she may evolve toward something celestial or deity-adjacent at the end. prep

Who knows

  • Nobody the party has met. Dreeshen knows only that the arrangement works; Ilna knows only "funding and equipment". prep
  • Barichello spoke with her across realms before he came; he was trying to reach God. He is starting to see she is not what she claimed. prep

How it could come out

  • The box's power signature matches one in Seranthis. S1
  • The Unbowed widened the gala job "on the Benefactor's behalf" (S12 prep version).
  • Flipping or interrogating Dreeshen's agents; Dreeshen himself, if reached.
  • Barichello, if found, has watched her longer than anyone.
  • The Oss'kai K'Tinga know more about the threat than anyone.
