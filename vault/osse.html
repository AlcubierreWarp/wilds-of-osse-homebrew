What it is

  • The continent where Gabriele was shipwrecked. S1

  • Settled about 250 years ago by exiles from Aglarond in Faerûn, on Toril. prep
  • Warped since the Spellplague of 1385 DR: magical creatures, change circles, roads cut through living wilderness. prep
