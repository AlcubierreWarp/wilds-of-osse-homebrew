What it is

  • A broadsheet of Althon's Gate. Headlines on Tom's page: prep
    • Arcane Marshals expand anti-cheating patrols for tournament season; Rhys warns about counterfeit black-market runes outside Gravenhurst.
    • Cassian Aerrowyn hospitalized after a containment circle collapsed at the Silverglass Annex; wardwrights say circles do not simply let go.
    • Northern border towns petition the Crown after disciplined raids burned their grain (Mayor Hadrin Voss).
    • A convoy near the Ashen Peaks was stalked by a smoke-serpent in the fog and saved by lean, painted, silent figures who took no thanks.
    • The "Sundermark Brides" ballad sparks brawls; pamphlets at dawn; two street printers vanished after visits from "inspectors".
    • Gravenhurst Dueling College disinvites Master Ilvarin Thorne for arguing the two kingdoms must cooperate or reunite.
    • Shrinekeepers report an unseasonal midsummer Spellplague flare; bay crossings toward the barrens sealed.
    • The Mid-Summer Festival opens the month-long build-up to the Royal Tournament (Deputy Provost Selric Marn).
