Session Log

SessionUpdates
S1Interrogated Gabriele under a truth-detection device and judged the story true. Disclosed the box holds enough power to level Althon's Gate and that a matching signature sits in Seranthis. Paid Belaskro's reclamation fee over Kallisch's objection, authorized GUS's repair, and named him from the surviving letters G-U-S. Recruited all three onto the poor-quarter missing-persons lead.
S2Sent up the distress flare during the siege and held the windows with Belaskro. Found the passage behind the tapestry and led the evacuation through the tunnels to the basilica.
S3Briefed the party on Seranthis, the 250-year sundering and the Sundermark Brides. Privately ordered GUS to watch Gabriele and detain him only for acts against the mission or public safety. Extended Belaskro's contract by two gold, then left to report to his captain.
S4Present at Marshal-Captain Rhys's briefing when the party was hired off the books. Tasked with educating GUS on discretion.
S5Briefed the party: black money, no record, full disavowal if caught. Played bad cop on Dask Corrin, then released him on a ruined-prosecution pretext so the party could tail him. At the Fair Maiden, Ilna Vesk shot a crossbow at him and missed; he chose not to arrest her. Disclosed his Pyreborn raid requests keep being denied by the King's Council.
S6Briefed the party that exploding shield runes are behind the tournament attacks and sent them to Silverglass Annex. Told GUS Marshal Intelligence can disguise a construct for undercover work. Found a receipt in Ser Ander Voss's locker for five runes from a maker other than Vantry, and the blank tablet where Vantry's floor supervisor vanished. Went along with Gabriele's arson plan.
S7Recognised Princess Timbit's rank and asked for her discretion, warning that the King's Council leans on the Marshals. Ran the fire response above ground.
S9Council orders put every Marshal on the Kilnworks fire. Deputized Belaskro as a contracted constable to keep the case going, named him GUS's conscience, and routed enforcement on Gabriele through him. Accepted Timbit onto the team. Passed on Corvane's gala invitation.
S12At the gala in dress uniform. Charged across the hall to stand over Garrick Moravel, was nicked by a poisoned blade, then killed by the last Knife on the floor. Looked to GUS and raised a hand as he died.
S13Revived by Gabriele's revivify within the minute (was dead since S12); healed 15. Called Gabriele a friend and freed him from supervision.
