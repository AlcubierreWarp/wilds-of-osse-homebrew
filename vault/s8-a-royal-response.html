S8 - A Royal Response

Session

The Kilnworks burns until the mage brigade comes; Queen Sabine and Marquis Corvane meet with open hatred; Corvane warns Belaskro off; Pell Marrow names the Drover's Rest; GUS cuffs Gabriele

What happened

  • Bucket lines under Malachai Wrent cannot hold the fire in The Kilnworks; red-robed brigade mages pull a river from the sea, and one of them combusts mid-cast.
  • A second rune building's sabotaged shield runes fail in the heat, letting fire through before it implodes.
  • Sabine Varenthal arrives unusually fast and promises Crown money to rebuild and strengthen the tournament industry.
  • Mid-speech she falters on seeing Corvane Aerrowyn in the crowd; Gabriele d'Avidicci and GUS see Corvane stare back with deep hatred.
  • Corvane takes Belaskro Tich aside: he knows of the investigation, tells him to stop, says he cannot protect him, offers him work, and invites him to dinner tomorrow to meet Cassian Aerrowyn, a competitor hurt in the attacks.
  • Ovett Kallisch heals GUS. GUS briefly detains a rude wood elf, Graydon Lightfoot.
  • Pell Marrow, now named, tells the party the Pyreborn hold his wife and son at The Drover's Rest on the southeast side: guards in the house and the stables, a large dog, a green light that turns red at a whistle.
  • GUS writes to the local Marshal dispatch suggesting a rescue "would not impede" the investigation.
  • In a dockside tavern a patron scorns the nobles and their hoarded runes; the room sings "The Sundermark Brides".
  • Morning: Gabriele sets off alone for the safe house; GUS chases him with grease and the steel defender and cuffs him a second time.

What changed

  • New: Sabine Varenthal, Corvane Aerrowyn met in person, Cassian Aerrowyn named as injured competitor, The Drover's Rest; Pell Marrow named.
  • Several more The Kilnworks buildings burned; one brigade mage dead.
  • Belaskro Tich invited to dinner at Aerrowyn Manor.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci in GUS's arcane cuffs.
  • Threads opened: Corvane and the Queen, The Kilnworks fire inquiry. Advanced: Pell Marrow's family (location known), The noble house behind the unrest.

Clues

  • ⚑ Vantry Runeworks is lost; the line only saves its neighbours: landed (more buildings lost before the brigade).
  • ⚑ Brigade arrives regardless: landed.
  • ⚑ Corvane clocks Belaskro and warns him off privately: landed.
  • ⚑ Queen Sabine arrives publicly and promises to fund rebuilding: landed.
  • ⚑ The Queen and Corvane surprised to see each other: landed (her falter, his hatred).
  • ⚑ Pell Marrow gives up the safe house: landed.
  • ⚑ Pell's wife and son rescued, or the cost of failing: not landed (party split over it).
  • Second runeworks fails with the Annex signature: landed.
  • Casualty count and the party's exposure for the fire: partly landed (one mage dead; blame not raised).

Plans

  • Fire line, Corvane, the Queen and the aftermath played much as planned, though Corvane came after the Queen and mentioned Cassian freely. The safe-house rescue did not happen: Gabriele's solo run ended in GUS's cuffs.

Open going into S9

  • Rescue Pell's family before the Pyreborn move them.
  • Dinner with Corvane and Cassian tomorrow.
  • Why does Corvane hate the Queen, and why did she come so fast?
