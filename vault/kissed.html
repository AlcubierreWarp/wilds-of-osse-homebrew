Kissed

also Kissed Blades, the Knives' poison
Lore · termfirst seen S12●●●●●

The Knives' poison condition: a nick fells the target, and only real curing brings them back

What it is

  • A mere nick from a Knife's blade dropped Éclair, Garrick and Aurelian in the first moments of the gala attack. S12
  • Timbit's lesser restoration cleared Éclair's poison and left her stable and unconscious. S12
  • Table rules: Kissed means unconscious; healing wakes but poison keeps biting; lesser restoration or protection from poison cures. The Hand carries Kissed Blades too. prep
