Who they are

  • Talking Persian cat of the visiting House Puss, investigating the sabotage because her cousin Éclair competes in the Royal Tournament. S7
  • White fur, blue eyes; carries herself as though the room were built for her. Darkvision. prep
  • Imperious; the world revolves around her and service is her due. prep
  • Flattery as a weapon: makes anyone feel the most important person in the room. prep
  • Vindictive. Being underestimated for her form is the fastest way to make her an enemy. prep
  • Lawful Evil methods with a sincerely charitable sense of noble duty. The gap is the comedy; lean on it. prep
  • Family in Meow: father King Croissant, grandmother Princess Chonkalot, letters only. Uncle Honey Cruller brought both girls across. prep
  • The royal line of Meow is cursed: all born in feline form. Wild Shape gives her any beast except her own humanoid shape. prep
  • Wildfire spirit; casts Eldritch Blast off Charisma via Spell Sniper, an open thread if Ally wants it. prep
  • Goals: find who tampers with the runes before Éclair is hurt; protect the family's standing; secretly, break the curse. prep
  • Temptation: whoever can credibly lift the curse owns her. Fear: a room where her name and rank mean nothing. prep
DM only
  • The cause of the curse is unknown to her. Candidates, unchosen: Spellplague residue that followed the line, an ancestor's bad bargain, or something that rhymes with the rune tampering. Keep it open; let Ossë supply it late. prep
  • A foreign princess's cousin in the running for the Varenthal throne is a lawful scandal, and someone at court has noticed. A motive aimed at Éclair, separate from the Pyreborn thread. prep
