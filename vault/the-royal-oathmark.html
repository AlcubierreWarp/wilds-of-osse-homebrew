The royal oathmark

also oathmark, royal oath, oath rolls
Lore · customprep●●●●●

Hidden mark under the skin of palace servants sworn for life to the royal family

What it is

  • Royal houses change with the tournament, so palace servants swear to the royal family itself. Leaving that service without leave is death; the trade is a life in the palace at the King's side. prep
  • The mark is hidden under the skin. Arcana DC 14 or detect magic finds it; History DC 12, or Timbit, names it. prep
  • A palace steward keeps the oath rolls. prep
DM only

The Hand who came for Cassian wore it: alive, bound and serving on the night. The loyal page who raised the dais shield wears it too. The mark says royal family, not which member. prep
