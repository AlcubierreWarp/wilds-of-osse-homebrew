Who is sabotaging shield runes to hurt Royal Tournament contenders, and to what end

Opened
S4

The question

  • Who is sabotaging the shield runes that protect tournament contenders, and what do they gain? S4

What the party knows

  • Elowen Rhys suspects the unrest aims at the Royal Tournament, the mageraft contest that confirms the crown prince. S4
  • Exploding shield runes are the main weapon; the Silverglass Annex rune was a Vantry Runeworks blank, finished elsewhere, carrying illusion magic. S6
  • Vantry lost a tenth of its blanks over two months with no break-ins. S6
  • Under Vantry Runeworks, a Pyreborn cell forged sabotaged runes with hostage labour: about 50 finished, 100 blanks. S7
  • A second rune building's runes failed the same way in the fire. S8
  • Cassian Aerrowyn and Tasmin Dreveth are among the hurt. S8, S11
  • The Council calls it quality control. S10
  • Sabine Varenthal confirmed counterfeit runes were planted, blamed foreign enemies, postponed the tournament two months and banned live training. S11
  • A Vantry blank pressed into a working shield drinks the whole spell. S12

Clues

  • Six fragments at the Annex, one Vantry with illusion magic. S6
  • Ellard Fenn's account: the shield let a bird through, then folded inward. S6
  • Ander Voss's receipt: five runes bought, six found. S6
  • Ordell Vantry's missing stock. S6
  • The hidden workshop and Pell Marrow's coerced crew. S7
  • Runes failing in the Kilnworks fire. S8
  • The Queen's speech. S11
  • The blank that swallowed the dais shield. S12
  • Pyreborn are one supply line, not the whole plot: not landed.
DM only
  • Sabine is the architect of the attacks on contenders; Cassian's containment-circle accident was hers. prep
  • Tasmin Dreveth's house has no tie to Cassian's circle: the attacks are broader than one house clearing a path. Dreveth is cash-poor and Tasmin bought a cheap rune. prep
  • Vantry is clean; he hid the stock losses to avoid a guild inquiry. prep
  • The Pyreborn sell to whoever pays; multiple actors are hitting the field. prep
