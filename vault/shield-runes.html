Shield runes

also shield rune, rune tablets, wardcraft, runes
Lore · magicfirst seen S6●●●●●

Magic poured into tablets that hold it; the counterfeit shield runes are how the tournament attacks work

What it is

  • Wrent briefed that exploding shield runes are the mechanism behind the tournament attacks. S6
  • At the Silverglass Annex a dome let a bird through, then folded inward in a white flash that burned two duellists and left the stone unmarked. GUS recovered six broken tablets: five stamped Kilnworks R Us, one Vantry with illusion magic layered over the shield work. S6
  • Broken shield runes collapse inward: the Annex, and the Kilnworks caving in as its runes woke in the fire. S6, S8
  • A rune is magic poured into something that can hold it; a blank is an empty tablet made to be filled. S11, Corvane from the dais
  • The Queen confirmed counterfeit runes were planted among challengers; stockpiles are near critical and the Kilnworks rebuild is the kingdom's priority. S11
  • A Vantry blank pressed into the royal page's dais dome siphoned the whole shield back into itself and freed Corvane. S12
DM only

Tasmin Dreveth bought her shield rune cheap, outside her house's usual channels, and has told no one. prep

The Knives' exit tablets were cut from blanks Pell Marrow skimmed for the Pyreborn. See The Knives used Corvane's blanks. prep
