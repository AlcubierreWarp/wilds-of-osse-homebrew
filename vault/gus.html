Who they are

  • Brass warforged investigator. His serial plate is scratched past reading except G-U-S; Wrent named him from it. S1
  • Bipedal, a little under average height, carved brass with steampunk clockwork. Slim tactile fingers, flip-down optical lenses at the side of the head. Short cape with Marshal colours and crest. prep
  • Pragmatic to the point of absurdity; everything black and white. Obeys Marshal direction because he has no reason to say no. "PRESENT CREDENTIALS OR I WILL REPORT YOU TO THE MARSHALS." prep
  • Flat and literal; says the quiet part. Social habits deliberately undeveloped: the place emerging sentience will show first. prep
  • Five to ten years old. Built by the Marshals as an investigator; has served Varenthal his whole life and never taken personal time. prep
  • Before S1: assigned to Wrent long enough that Wrent began to treat him as a person. Later wrecked by a wolverine-like dark beast on a field job and left for scrap by a dismissive Marshal ("we'll make another one"). prep
  • Constructs are second class: not slaves, not persons. The cape marks ownership as much as authority. The only unserialized construct in the Marshals. prep
  • Healing mends him like anyone else (table ruling). S8
  • Wants: to keep his purpose and climb the construct ranks. Secret want: to become a real person. prep
  • Fear: being scrapped or losing purpose. prep
  • Shane prefers political intrigue and factionalism. prep
DM only
  • Wrent was GUS's Marshal Intelligence handler, at Wrent's own request. prep
  • The dismissive Marshal who scrapped him is unnamed and unbuilt. Wrent is emphatically not that Marshal. prep
  • Wrent's revival did not restore the handler link. Under EP-07 GUS is his own handler; nobody knows. prep
  • Long arcs: cumulative construct abuse could make him a construct freedom fighter; a wish could make him organic. Meta: he stumbles into leadership among constructs, something he was never built to want. prep

S1Hauled into the Sanctuary as salvage by Belaskro. Five Marshal mages repaired him; Wrent named him Gus and recruited him.

S8Wrote to Marshal dispatch about the safe house. Chased down and cuffed Gabriele. Mended by Kallisch.

S12Shocked a prone Knife and sent for his steel defender, which struck the Vayne Knife. Heard "Handler Vitals: Disconnected" as Wrent died.
S13Heard "Handler signal lost" and followed EP-07; gives his name as Steven. Questioned Captain Dunmore and ordered roadblocks for Grey. Answered a stranger's contact phrase by the manor doors.
