S10 - The Aerrowyn Gala

Session

Gala prep at Tip's Top Tailors, Alma Marrow's stolen ledger pages, Timbit's ruse on Nessa Ruhl, and the party's entry to the Aerrowyn gala, where Belaskro meets Cassian

What happened

  • At Yorwick Dray's outpost the party rests; GUS recharges; Gabriele d'Avidicci and Princess Timbit raid the lost and found for gala wear.
  • Belaskro Tich buys a black velvet robe at Tip's Top Tailors; Tip embroiders it in rainbow florals (15 gp).
  • Alma Marrow brings torn pages from Nessa Ruhl's ledger: names, dates, payments, under the Pyreborn sun-and-moon crest.
  • Timbit flashes the signet ring and poses as a Pyreborn double agent sent by Bram: Nessa says the rune sabotage is part of "the plan" and that "the boss" means to control the city. Her charm person fails and Nessa sees through it.
  • Back at Tip's, Gabriele buys clerical robes (12 gp), GUS leaves a 10 gp IOU for a cape, and Gabriele's thaumaturgic farts get him banned.
  • At Aerrowyn Manor Timbit's title and the Pyreborn threat get the whole party in, GUS included.
  • Corvane Aerrowyn opens the giving at 10,000 gp for the Kilnworks, stressing runes for the kingdom's defence.
  • Walking Belaskro upstairs, Corvane says Maribel Aerrowyn died in childbirth nineteen years ago, and that the King's Council treats the rune failures as quality control unless tampering is proven.
  • Belaskro meets Cassian Aerrowyn, blonde, eighteen, healing, eager for frontier kin, and snide about Belaskro's "common" look.
  • Timbit names the houses present: House Aldric, House Moravel, House Halvenne (with Lady Odile, belle of the court), House Vayne, House Puss, and the Seranthis House Dreveth.

What changed

  • New: Tip, Tip's Top Tailors, Lady Odile; Cassian Aerrowyn met in person.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci banned from Tip's Top Tailors; GUS owes Tip 10 gp.
  • The party holds Nessa Ruhl's ledger pages.
  • Nessa Ruhl now knows Timbit is not Pyreborn.
  • Revealed: Corvane Aerrowyn sits on the King's Council; Maribel's maiden name is Sorne.
  • Threads advanced: The Pyreborn mark ("the boss" wants the city), The noble house behind the unrest.

Clues

  • ⚑ Pell reunited with Alma and Den: already landed in S9.
  • ⚑ Alma's ledger pages: landed.
  • ⚑ The pages name Bram Kell as the man who placed the Marrows: not landed (Nessa named "Bram" instead).
  • ⚑ Corvane's welcome speech: landed.
  • ⚑ Queen Sabine's speech, the moratorium and the two-month delay: not reached.
  • ⚑ The heckler on Crown rune stockpiles: not reached.
  • ⚑ Cassian attacked in his chambers; the Unbowed fist tattoo; Corvane's vow to fight in his place: not reached.

Plans

  • Station handoff and Corvane's speech played; the session ended with the party mingling before the Queen's address. Everything from the Queen's speech on carries forward.

Open going into S11

  • Timbit heading to the House Puss table.
  • Who is "the boss", and what is "the plan"?
  • Corvane on the Council while warning Belaskro off: protecting him or himself?
