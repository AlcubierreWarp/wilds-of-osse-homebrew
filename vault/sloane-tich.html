DM only

Alive. An Oss'kai K'Tinga hunting party pulled her from the spider's lair, recognised her Oss'kai blood and her mother, healed her, and adopted her into the vale.

Believes Belaskro and Aldous both died that day. Neither twin knows the other lived.

Her mother (the twins' Oss'kai mother) was with her for years, then left her life. She convinced Sloane that reaching out would expose Oss'kai magic, and kept her from going home.

Reunion thread for when the party reaches Oss'kai territory. If Belaskro pushes on Oss'kai secrets, he pushes against the promise that kept her away.
