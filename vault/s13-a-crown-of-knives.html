S13 - A Crown of Knives

Session

Gabriele revives Wrent, Corvane kills the Hand and throws the Queen out, Grey is named for the guard order, and a stranger greets GUS with a contact phrase

What happened

  • Gabriele d'Avidicci reaches Malachai Wrent within the minute and casts revivify through Barichello's rosary; the cross burns a cross-shaped mark into his palm. Wrent wakes: "What the fuck happened?"
  • Upstairs, Belaskro Tich holds The Hand off the bed while Cassian Aerrowyn throws fire from it. Corvane Aerrowyn bursts in, empowers Belaskro and caves the assassin's skull in. The last ward crystal fails.
  • Belaskro finds a royal oathmark behind the assassin's right ear; detect magic shows the royal house. Corvane: keep it between us. Later he tells the party it ties the man to the royal household.
  • Princess Timbit asks Sabine Varenthal for an enchanted frigate. "The crown was in your debt. I did not say it was bankrupt." Promised instead: the fastest sailboat, with cannons and a cat-mermaid figurehead, to be named the HMCS Ferdinand. "Someone will be in touch."
  • Gabriele gives holy-water rites over Garrick Moravel and Aurelian Aldric on the stage.
  • Captain Dunmore: the house guard went to the gates on an order under Corvane's seal, "so the Dreveth party wouldn't feel watched", handed over by Under-Steward Grey. Corvane: "I gave no such order." Only he and Grey can reach the seal. Grey's rooms are empty; guards run to close the city gates.
  • The musicians' staff list, adding five players, is signed "by order of Under-Steward Grey".
  • GUS hears "Handler signal lost" and follows EP-07. He now gives his name as Steven, care of Lt. Wrent at the Citadel.
  • Timbit reports that Yorwick Dray waved off her security worries before the gala. Corvane: "We will find out what happened to Sergeant Dray."
  • Sabine on the robe: "Robes are stolen every day. Grief makes poor evidence." Timbit's insight: shock and panic, then a glance up toward Cassian's room. Corvane: she knew about upstairs.
  • Corvane before the nobles with The blue robe: Cassian lives "because of my nephew"; "My son will never walk again"; "My son cannot stand, so I will stand for him." He accuses the Crown and orders: "Now get out of my house!" Sabine: "I gave that toast," but the actions that followed were not hers; the Marshals will have everything she can give. Leaving: "Princess Timbit, the crown remembers its debt." Corvane keeps the robe.
  • Wrent, healed by Gabriele, calls him a friend: "You could have let me die, and your bonds would have been broken regardless." He lifts Gabriele's supervision: a free man.
  • Jared Dreveth: House Dreveth was set up to take the fall and leaves at mid-morning. He invites Gabriele to Seranthis, calls Varenthal "swine", and gives a city address and a contact: ask for Shane Dawson at the South Gate. He has never heard of Father Barichello.
  • Timbit, curled in Lady Odile's lap: "The shield went up first." Elias "was never in danger"; the dead champions are "no one who matters".
  • Honey Cruller confines Timbit to the Embassy of the Kingdom of Meow until there is a name; Gabriele goes as her bodyguard. Belaskro takes the guest suite across from Cassian. GUS stands in a corner by the doors.
  • A passer-by: "Fine weather for the season." GUS: "Every season has weather." Cut to black.

What changed

  • Malachai Wrent alive again (was dead).
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci free of supervision; Wrent's friend.
  • The Hand dead, killed by Corvane. Cassian's ward crystals all spent.
  • Noémie Vayne stabilized by a House Vayne cleric. Éclair Exquisite woke, furious at being saved.
  • Grey fled, hunted. Dunmore new.
  • Corvane Aerrowyn publicly accused the Crown and threw the Queen out; he keeps The blue robe.
  • Timbit, Gabriele, Éclair and Honey at the Embassy of the Kingdom of Meow. Sabine Varenthal gone from the manor.
  • Revealed: The Hand served the royal family, Grey forged the guard order.
  • New: HMCS Ferdinand (promised), Shane Dawson, South Gate, Embassy of the Kingdom of Meow.
  • Threads advanced: The champions' assassins, Corvane and the Queen, The Crown's debt to Timbit, GUS's secret file, Find Father Barichello.
