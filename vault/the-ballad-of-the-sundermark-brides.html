The Ballad of the Sundermark Brides

also Sundermark Brides, The Sundermark Brides, Ballad of the Sundermark Brides
Lore · songfirst seen S3●●●●●

Folk ballad of a Seranthis prince, an Oss'kai huntress and a burned vale; sung bawdy in taverns, mournful on the border

What it is

  • Twelve verses and a chorus. A young prince flees his father's crown, is saved in the barrens from a Spellplague beast by a huntress, and lives with her in a vale "where healers sing". His father marches on the vale, burns it, kills the children and the huntress, takes the women as brides in chains, and jails the prince. His brother takes the throne. prep
  • Sung two ways: fast and loud in Sundermark's taverns, chorus shouted; slow and unaccompanied among the Oss'kai and the border towns, final chorus spoken. prep
  • At the Glowing Goat the bartender told the party its dark history; Wrent framed it as Seranthis taking Oss'kai women by force as brides during the Sundering. S3
  • The Unbowed and the Pyreborn were both using the song to stir trouble, per Rhys. S4
  • A tavern crowd sang it in front of the party after the Kilnworks fire. S8
  • The Althon's Gate dockside version mocks Seranthis, indigenous peoples and mixed families; brawls, dawn pamphlets, vanished printers. prep: Varenthal Voice
DM only

The Unbowed seed the ballad and engineer the brawls; it is one of Dreeshen Vale's fingerprints in Althon's Gate. prep

The Pyreborn are not the Sundermark Brides crew: keep that thread apart from the Barichello and Seranthis chain. prep, Belaskro&#x27;s page

The vale "where healers sing and the mountain's wounds run clean" fits the Oss'kai Mage Healers. Tom has not said so; treat as an open echo. prep
