Belaskro's Oss'kai heritage

Secrethidden from the partyprep●●●●●

Belaskro is half-Oss'kai through the mother who left the twins on his father's doorstep

Known by
Sloane Tich, Oss'kai K'Tinga

The truth

  • Belaskro's mother was Oss'kai. After a brief relationship with Aldous Tich she left Belaskro and his twin Sloane on Aldous's doorstep and went. Belaskro does not know. prep
  • His sharp features are the tell; he is not a strong enough magic user for his hair to bleach. prep
  • Same thread as his long-term goal of finding his mother, and his planned multiclass into Paladin (Oath of the Ancients) as latent magic wakes. prep
  • The mother may have other children. Acceptance or rejection, late game. Not built yet. prep

Who knows

  • Sloane knows her own Oss'kai blood, from the K'Tinga who took her in. prep
  • The Oss'kai K'Tinga recognized Sloane's blood and her mother; they will likely see it in Belaskro before he does. prep
  • The mother, who has no note yet. prep

How it could come out

  • The K'Tinga recognize him on sight in the wilds.
  • Meeting Sloane, or her ring.
  • Latent magic manifesting toward the Oath of the Ancients.
  • Noémie Vayne, who hides her own Oss'kai blood, bonded with him over the Oss'kai S11; a second look from her.
  • Dreeshen Vale's Oss'kai-adjacent contacts may cross his mother's trail (flagged, not built).
