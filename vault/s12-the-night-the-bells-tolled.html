S12 - The Night the Bells Tolled

Session

The Knives strike the champions at the Queen's toast: Garrick Moravel, Aurelian Aldric and Lt. Wrent die, Timbit frees Corvane with a blank rune, and Belaskro reaches Cassian's room

What happened

  • The disguised musicians Misty Step onto the floor: by the end of round zero Éclair Exquisite, Garrick Moravel and Aurelian Aldric are down from a nick of poisoned blade; Noémie Vayne is wounded; Rowan Halvenne shoves his assassin prone.
  • "Father!" from the stair to Cassian Aerrowyn's wing: a second attack upstairs.
  • Princess Timbit feeds Belaskro Tich goodberries and crosses to Éclair; Belaskro runs for the stairs; GUS sends his homunculus for the steel defender.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci pulls Jared Dreveth under a table and sets St. Peter on Noémie's attacker. His read: House Dreveth's table had no assassin, so someone is staging the blame.
  • Honey Cruller leaps onto the face of Éclair's assassin. Malachai Wrent, in dress uniform, defends the fallen Garrick and is nicked. Noémie falls to the poison. Aurelian's killer dimension-doors out.
  • A bell tolls each round and a ward around Cassian's room cracks; Corvane Aerrowyn, trapped behind a royal page's shield bubble on the dais, shouts "Faster! There's only five left!"
  • Gabriele throws Timbit one of Belaskro's Vantry blanks; she pushes it through the bubble's hole into Corvane's hand and the blank drinks the whole shield, freeing him.
  • Sabine Varenthal: "Whoever you are, the crown is in your debt." Timbit answers with her full title. The marksmen dimension-door out of the gallery.
  • The last floor assassin kills Wrent. In GUS's head: "Handler Vitals: Disconnected." Wrent's last gesture to GUS: don't worry, I've got this.
  • Timbit's lesser restoration stabilizes Éclair, unconscious.
  • Upstairs, a blue-robed assassin is held off by six crystals, five nearly spent. Belaskro body-checks him from the bed and marks him; "You're not on the contract. Leave, and I'll forget you." Corvane storms up the stairs as the session ends.

What changed

  • Malachai Wrent dead; GUS's handler link lost.
  • Garrick Moravel and Aurelian Aldric dead.
  • Éclair Exquisite poisoned, stabilized, unconscious. Noémie Vayne down, fate open.
  • Cassian Aerrowyn's wards nearly gone; Belaskro between him and the assassin.
  • One of Belaskro's blanks used: it absorbed a shield spell whole.
  • The Crown owes Princess Timbit a debt, in the Queen's words.
  • New: the Knives (the gala assassins), the blue-robed assassin.
  • Threads opened: The Crown's debt to Timbit. Advanced: The champions' assassins, GUS's secret file, Corvane and the Queen.

Plans

  • Ran to the plan's round structure. Garrick and Aurelian died and Wrent fell; Corvane was freed by Timbit's hand rather than an Arcana roll. The fight upstairs and the whole aftermath carry into S13.

Open going into S13

  • Noémie Vayne's fate.
  • Who hired the Knives, and who signed the contract on Cassian?
  • GUS without his handler.
