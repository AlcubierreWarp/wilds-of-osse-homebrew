S7 - Forged in Fire

Session

Under Vantry Runeworks the party and the newly met Princess Timbit break a Pyreborn rune-forging cell, a dying man mistakes Belaskro for the Pyreborn leader, and the fire takes the district

What happened

  • A spiral stair behind the wall drops hundreds of feet to a hidden workshop under Vantry Runeworks: six workers and the gnome supervisor (Pell Marrow) forging sabotaged shield runes from stolen blanks.
  • The gnome confirms the crew's families are held hostage to keep them working.
  • Princess Timbit of House Puss, a talking Persian cat, arrives investigating the sabotage for her sister-cousin Éclair Exquisite, who is in the Royal Tournament.
  • Malachai Wrent asks her discretion, then goes up to fight the fire.
  • The workers torch the machines and run; the gnome surrenders and tackles one of them.
  • GUS's acid and Timbit's wildfire and poison win the fight. Gabriele d'Avidicci smothers a burning machine with his body. GUS is dropped by a mace on the stairs.
  • Belaskro Tich whips a fleeing worker, who dies saying Belaskro "looks just like him", the leader of the Pyreborn, and names "the Marquis"; only Belaskro hears it. (Tom's ruling, 2026-11-18)
  • Timbit finds a gold sun-and-moon signet ring, a mark of high Pyreborn rank, on one body; the party takes two finished sabotaged runes and blanks.
  • About 50 finished sabotaged runes and 100 blanks were in the workshop.
  • They drag GUS up into an alley: the diversion fire has destroyed Vantry Runeworks and is spreading through The Kilnworks; Wrent runs the firefight.

What changed

  • New: Princess Timbit joins the party; Éclair Exquisite and House Puss named.
  • Vantry Runeworks destroyed by fire; the fire spreads through The Kilnworks.
  • The six workers killed or captured; one, a Pyreborn ranker, dead.
  • Princess Timbit holds the Pyreborn signet ring and two sabotaged runes.
  • Threads opened: Pell Marrow's family. Advanced: The Pyreborn mark (the leader's face), The tournament sabotage.

Clues

  • ⚑ Crescent moon in a sun on a counterfeiting document: landed as the signet ring, not a document.
  • The Pyreborn are one supply line, not the whole plot: not landed explicitly.
  • Workers' families held hostage, "one more month": landed.
  • The dying worker mistakes Belaskro for the Pyreborn leader: landed.
  • Pell Marrow's wife and son at a safe house on the edge of town: not landed (no location yet).

Plans

  • Played the stowaway and Under-Storage scenes. Timbit entered at the workshop rather than behind the pallets. No paperwork with the crest was found; a ring carried that beat. The party's fire went district-wide.

Open going into S8

  • Is anyone dead in the Kilnworks fire, and who gets blamed?
  • Where are the hostage families?
  • Who is the Pyreborn leader who looks like Belaskro?
