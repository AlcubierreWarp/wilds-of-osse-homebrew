S11 - To the Champions of Varenthal

Session

The party works the gala's houses, learns Nessa Ruhl was freed, GUS is recruited by Marshal intelligence, and the Queen's toast is the signal for assassins to fall on the champions

What happened

  • Honey Cruller tells Princess Timbit two factions are forming: Corvane's pro-tournament camp and the Crown loyalists. He asks her to spy on the loyalists; his whiskers itch near one house.
  • At the House Halvenne table, Lady Odile (annoyed by her brother Rowan Halvenne) whispers that she is promised to Prince Elias Varenthal if he keeps his crown-prince place in the tournament; not public.
  • Yorwick Dray arrives with a black eye: brigands freed Nessa Ruhl from her cage; she asked if "the plan" was still on and they said yes.
  • Vigil, as "Steven", unlocks an encrypted file in GUS's memory: a secret Marshal intelligence service watching Seranthis interference in the tournament. GUS reports the Ambassador's talk.
  • GUS's scarab homunculus guards the party's untouched food; Timbit warns them not to eat or drink on return.
  • Noémie Vayne, the Vayne champion, bonds with Belaskro Tich over frontier hunts and the Oss'kai, backs Corvane's ideals, and gets him tipsy.
  • Timbit charms Tasmin Dreveth in her wheelchair over shared dislike of Éclair Exquisite.
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci lets Barichello's rosary fall before Jared Dreveth and his lady: no recognition at all (insight 19).
  • Corvane Aerrowyn announces gifts from House Dreveth (8,000 gp) and House Vayne (12,000 gp).
  • Sabine Varenthal confirms counterfeit runes were planted, glances at House Dreveth as she blames foreign enemies, postpones the Royal Tournament two months, bans live combat training, and promises Crown reserves stay sealed, even for her son.
  • At her toast "to the champions", someone cries "Father!" in the manor and the balcony musicians teleport into the crowd with daggers raised.

What changed

  • New: Honey Cruller, Noémie Vayne, Tasmin Dreveth, Jared Dreveth, Rowan Halvenne, Vigil (as "Steven"); Prince Elias Varenthal named.
  • Nessa Ruhl freed by brigands (was caged at Dray's outpost).
  • GUS recruited into Marshal intelligence contact; new file unlocked in his memory.
  • Royal Tournament postponed two months; live combat training banned.
  • Threads opened: The champions' assassins, GUS's secret file. Advanced: The tournament sabotage, The Pyreborn mark (Nessa freed), Find Father Barichello (House Dreveth's leaders not involved).

Clues

  • ⚑ Presentation of the champions and Corvane's toast to Cassian's empty place: not landed.
  • ⚑ Table-hopping between courses: landed.
  • ⚑ The Queen's speech, moratorium and two-month delay: landed.
  • ⚑ END on "To the champions of Varenthal", blades from above, Cassian's scream: landed (cry of "Father!", musicians with daggers).
  • Lady Odile's betrothal to Elias: landed.
  • Heckler on Crown stockpiles: not landed; the Queen offered the stockpile promise unprompted.
  • Sabine and Corvane each think the other hurt Cassian: not landed.

Plans

  • The gala ran close to plan: the champions' presentation, pledge war and parlour flourishes were skipped, while Nessa's rescue, Steven's approach to GUS and the Dreveth rosary test came from play.

Open going into S12

  • Assassins among the champions, mid-toast.
  • Who cried "Father!", and is Cassian under attack upstairs?
  • Nessa Ruhl loose, and "the plan" still on.
