Marquis Corvane Aerrowyn is the secret boss of the Pyreborn; Bram Kell works for him

Known by
Belaskro Tich, Corvane Aerrowyn, Bram Kell, Sabine Varenthal, Corwin Thatch

The truth

  • Corvane heads House Aerrowyn and secretly runs the Pyreborn. Bram Kell is his on-scene leader. prep
  • The gang is an instrument; he will cut it loose the moment it is a liability. prep
  • Sabine knows the Pyreborn are his and cannot move openly; he suspects the Crown and cannot say so. A cold war neither can acknowledge. prep
  • He held Pell Marrow's wife and son to force a month of skimming at Vantry. prep

Who knows

  • Belaskro, alone: a dying worker named "the Marquis" as leader. S7 No other PC heard it.
  • Bram Kell, Sabine, and Corwin Thatch (who knows the Pyreborn-Aerrowyn link). prep
  • Rhys suspects a great house and has no evidence. prep

How it could come out

  • Belaskro tells the party.
  • The brand, the ring and the ledger all point at Bram, and Bram points at Corvane.
  • Nessa Ruhl talks for terms; she named "the boss" S10.
  • Corwin Thatch, pressed.
