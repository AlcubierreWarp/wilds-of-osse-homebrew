Why the Pyreborn wear the moon-and-sun crest from Corvane's wagon, who leads them, and what 'the plan' is

Opened
S2

The question

  • Why do the Pyreborn wear the crest from Belaskro's family past, who is their leader, and what is "the plan"? S2

What the party knows

  • Bram Kell wears a crescent moon in a sun, the mark on the wagon that took Belaskro's uncle to Althon's Gate. S2
  • The mark stands for the union of Corvane Aerrowyn's family (sun) and Maribel Aerrowyn's (moon); Maribel gave Belaskro and Sloane Tich moon and sun rings. S3
  • Elowen Rhys believes a Varenthal noble house directs the Pyreborn. S4
  • Bram Kell leads them from a hovel ten minutes east of the mission; they outnumber the Unbowed ten to one; the King's Council blocks every raid. S5
  • A Pyreborn cell forged sabotaged runes under Vantry Runeworks with hostage labour. S7
  • A gold sun-and-moon signet ring marks high rank; Princess Timbit wears it. S7
  • A dying Pyreborn told Belaskro Tich he "looks just like him", the leader of the Pyreborn. S7
  • Nessa Ruhl: the sabotage is part of "the plan", and "the boss" means to control the city. She spoke of Bram. S10
  • Brigands freed Nessa and told her "the plan" was still on. S11

Clues

  • Bram's crest matches the wagon. S2
  • The flashback and the rings. S3
  • Ilna's intelligence on Bram and the hovel. S5
  • The signet ring. S7
  • The dying man's words, heard only by Belaskro. S7
  • Nessa Ruhl's ledger pages under the crest, from Alma Marrow. S10
  • Nessa's boast about "the boss". S10
  • Nessa's rescue: "the plan is still on". S11
  • The ledger pages name Bram Kell as the man who placed the Marrows: not landed.
DM only
  • Corvane is behind the Pyreborn: the Vantry cellar was his operation, and the Pyreborn are his supply line. prep The dying man named "the Marquis" to Belaskro alone. (S7, Tom's ruling)
  • Belaskro has the face of Corvane's dead brother. prep
  • The Pyreborn sell sabotaged runes to whoever pays; they are one supply line, not the whole tournament plot. prep
  • Corvane knows the Drover's Rest fell and says nothing unless Belaskro pushes. prep
