Grey forged the guard order

also The forged order, Grey's guard order
Secretrevealed S13prep●●●●●

Under-Steward Grey used Corvane's own seal, without his knowledge, to pull the Aerrowyn guards off the doors, and has vanished

Known by
Grey, Dunmore, Corvane Aerrowyn

The truth

  • Under-Steward Grey handed Captain Dunmore an order under Corvane's seal sending the house guard to the gates, "so the Dreveth party wouldn't feel watched". Corvane gave no such order: Grey used the real seal without his knowledge. S13
  • Grey signed the hiring chit for the extra musicians. Nobody has seen him since the toast. prep
  • He was bought through a family relation. Who paid: the S12 plan says the Unbowed; the S13 plan leaves it deliberately open so the hunt can go anywhere. prep

Who knows

  • Grey and whoever paid him. prep
  • The party, Corvane and Captain Dunmore, from Dunmore's testimony. S13

How it could come out

  • Captain Dunmore's testimony; the order with Corvane's own seal.
  • The hiring chit on the gallery.
  • GUS's EP-07 standing order, and Corvane's ask to Belaskro.
  • Grey's family relation.
