S1 - Strangers in Ossë

Session

Gabriele wakes in the Sanctuary, Belaskro brings in GUS, and Wrent leads them to the Poor-Quarter Mission after Father Barichello

What happened

  • Cold open: Gabriele d'Avidicci aboard the Santa Marie under Alonso de Vergara, carrying a locked box for the Pope; a red-glowing storm wrecks the ship.
  • Gabriele wakes in a cell in The Sanctuary in Althon's Gate, beside Corwin Thatch.
  • Malachai Wrent interrogates him with a truth disc: the box holds enough power to level the city, and a matching signature has been read in Kingdom of Seranthis.
  • Praying for Corwin, Gabriele casts guidance without meaning to.
  • Belaskro Tich hauls in the wrecked GUS; Wrent overrules Ovett Kallisch, pays, and hires Belaskro.
  • Five Marshal mages repair GUS; only G-U-S is legible on his plate, so Wrent names him Gus.
  • A missing-persons report matching Father Barichello leads the group to the Poor-Quarter Mission.
  • Lux Aveline Corstane recalls a "Christian" man who vanished two weeks ago.
  • A beggar prays over a rosary with a strange symbol; GUS detects odd magic; a nervous cleric flees.
  • A mob under a local boss (Bram Kell, unnamed yet) surrounds the mission. The party barricades in; GUS's homunculus reports "item found".

What changed

  • New: Malachai Wrent, Corwin Thatch, Ovett Kallisch, Lux Aveline Corstane, Alonso de Vergara (cold open only).
  • GUS repaired and back online; Belaskro Tich hired by the Arcane Marshals.
  • The locked box is held by the Marshals at The Sanctuary.
  • Threads opened: Find Father Barichello, Purpose of the locked box.

Clues

  • ⚑ The box was found near Gabriele, not on him: landed (Wrent believes him).
  • ⚑ The box reads as catastrophically powerful: landed.
  • ⚑ The box's signature matches a Seranthis reading on file: landed.
  • ⚑ Gabriele matches a missing-foreigner file (Barichello): landed through the mission report.
  • ⚑ Barichello dragged off by the "Sundermark Brides" ballad crew: not landed.
  • ⚑ The out-of-place rosary confirms Barichello: partly landed (seen on a beggar; GUS's homunculus found an item).
  • Wrent's held card on Gabriele's clothing: not played, as planned.

Plans

  • Plan ended at the mission with the ballad-crew clue; play ended mid-scene in a siege by Bram's mob, which the plan did not include.

Open going into S2

  • Trapped in the burning-to-be mission by a hostile mob.
  • What did GUS's homunculus find?
  • Where is Father Barichello?
