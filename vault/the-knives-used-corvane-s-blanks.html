The Knives used Corvane's blanks

also The batch stamp
Secrethidden from the partyprep●●●●●

The Knives' exit tablets were cut from Vantry blanks Pell Marrow skimmed for the Pyreborn

The truth

  • The snapped tablets the Knives used to leave the gala carry a batch stamp: Vantry blanks Pell Marrow skimmed for the Pyreborn. Corvane's own supply line built the exits for the people who came for his son. prep

Who knows

  • Nobody has put it together. Pell knows he skimmed blanks; he does not know where they went. prep

How it could come out

  • Investigation DC 14 on the snapped blanks: the same stock as Belaskro's.
  • Pell Marrow, asked about batches.
  • Whoever moved the blanks from the Pyreborn to the Unbowed.
