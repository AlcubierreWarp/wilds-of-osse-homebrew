The question

  • Will the Kilnworks fire be traced to the party, and who will be blamed for it? S8

What the party knows

  • Gabriele d'Avidicci threw the torch into Vantry Runeworks over GUS's warning that a death would be a capital offence. S6
  • The fire took the Runeworks and spread across The Kilnworks. S7
  • A brigade mage combusted dragging the sea into the streets. S8
  • Corvane Aerrowyn gave Belaskro veiled warnings about the fire's cause. S8
  • The King's Council put every Marshal onto the fire investigation. S9
  • The Crown pledged to rebuild; Corvane's gala raised 10,000 gp (Aerrowyn), 8,000 (Dreveth) and 12,000 (Vayne). S8, S10, S11

Clues

  • GUS's warning. S6
  • The district-wide spread. S7
  • The dead brigade mage; Corvane's warning. S8
  • The Council's order. S9
