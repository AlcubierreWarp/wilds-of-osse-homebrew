The Hand served the royal family

Secretrevealed S13prep●●●●●

The assassin in Cassian's room was a sworn palace servant of the royal family

Known by
Sabine Varenthal, Belaskro Tich, Corvane Aerrowyn, Cassian Aerrowyn

The truth

  • The Hand was alive, oath-bound and serving the royal family on the night. He came for Cassian alone. prep
  • Ties to the Crown: the hidden oathmark, a genuine palace robe, and Sabine's morning order that had the dais shield ready and penned Corvane away from the stairs. prep
  • The mark says royal family. It does not say which member: Queen, King or Prince stays open for the party. prep Who sent him: The Queen sent the Hand.
  • Conflict for Tom: the S12 plan says Sabine hired the Hand through Unbowed middlemen ("the middleman who sold her the Hand"). The S13 plan makes him a sworn royal servant sent under the Unbowed attack's cover. This note follows S13.

Who knows

  • Sabine. prep
  • Belaskro found the mark with Cassian there; Corvane told the party it ties the man to the royal household. S13

How it could come out

  • "You're not on the contract" S12: he was there for one target.
  • The oathmark on his body (Arcana DC 14 or detect magic; History DC 12 or Timbit names it).
  • The blue robe; no Unbowed fist tattoo on him, unlike the Knives.
  • The loyal page who wears the same mark and raised the shield on the Queen's order.
  • The palace steward who keeps the oath rolls.
