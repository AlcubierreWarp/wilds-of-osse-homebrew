DM only

Corvane believes in the constitution and wants the crown kept contestable. The Pyreborn are his instrument; Queen Sabine is the opposing hand. Cassian is kept clean.

The Pyreborn brand is the old Aerrowyn warrior-clan mark; the gang financed the house's rise through contraband wards.

Under-Steward Grey forged the order that pulled the house guard off the gala doors; the Unbowed bought him through a family relation (S12 plan, not landed).
