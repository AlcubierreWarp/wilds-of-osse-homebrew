S2 - Mission Under Fire

Session

The party escapes Bram Kell's Pyreborn mob as the mission burns, with Barichello's prayer book, a strange rosary and Rosalia's offer of answers

What happened

  • Malachai Wrent fires a distress flare; Gabriele d'Avidicci searches the Poor-Quarter Mission, arming himself with a rusty rapier and a wooden shield.
  • Belaskro Tich sees the mob leader's crescent-moon-in-a-sun mark: the same mark was on the wagon that took his uncle and aunt to Althon's Gate when he was twelve.
  • The mob stacks hay against the walls; GUS douses it with acid; Bram Kell lights it with a firebolt.
  • Fight in the street and from the windows as the mission burns.
  • Gabriele finds Father Barichello's prayer book, heavily annotated in his hand.
  • GUS takes a rosary with unfamiliar magic from the altar into his evidence compartment.
  • Bram breaks in shouting "For the Pyreborn!"
  • Wrent finds the secret tunnel Lux Aveline Corstane was guarding; Belaskro's fog cloud covers the escape.
  • They surface by a basilica where Lux introduces Rosalia Whitlock, who says she knows what happened to Barichello.

What changed

  • New: Bram Kell (named as Bram), Rosalia Whitlock (as Rosalia), the Pyreborn (named).
  • Poor-Quarter Mission set on fire.
  • Gabriele holds Barichello's prayer book; GUS holds the altar rosary.
  • Threads opened: The Pyreborn mark. Siege escaped.

Clues

  • Pyreborn mark matches the wagon from Belaskro's past: landed.
  • Barichello's annotated prayer book: landed.
  • Rosary with unusual magic: landed (in GUS's evidence compartment).
  • Rosalia knows what happened to Barichello: landed as a lead.

Plans

  • Played the siege scene. The Everlight Hospital visit to the Champion and the Glowing Goat scene were not reached; Rosalia's tip carries into S3.

Open going into S3

  • What does Rosalia know about Barichello?
  • Who are the Pyreborn, and what is their mark to Belaskro's family?
  • What is the box?
