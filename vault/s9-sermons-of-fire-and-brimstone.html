S9 - Sermons of Fire and Brimstone

Session

Wrent deputizes Belaskro after the Council pulls the Marshals off the case; the party raids the Drover's Rest, frees Pell Marrow's family and captures Pyreborn lieutenant Nessa Ruhl

What happened

  • GUS marches a cuffed Gabriele d'Avidicci into Yorwick Dray's station; Princess Timbit talks Dray into removing the cuffs; GUS ropes Gabriele to the steel defender instead.
  • Malachai Wrent arrives: the King's Council has ordered every Marshal onto the Kilnworks fire, leaving the sabotage case unsupported.
  • Wrent deputizes Belaskro Tich as a constable-contractor with full authority, makes him GUS's "conscience", and takes Timbit onto the team.
  • Wrent passes on Belaskro's formal invitation to Corvane Aerrowyn's fundraising gala for the Kilnworks that evening.
  • At The Drover's Rest, Timbit befriends the abused guard dog Gabe with speak with animals and a beast bond; Belaskro picks his chain.
  • Gabriele preaches fire and brimstone across the street as a distraction. Gabe reports four guards, Alma Marrow in the common room and her son Den in the cellar.
  • Belaskro knocks over a log pile and the Pyreborn come out fighting.
  • A convert from Gabriele's crowd joins in with a shoehorn and is cut down by lieutenant Nessa Ruhl.
  • GUS's homunculus and defender, Timbit's claws and Belaskro's crossbow kill the guards; Belaskro brings Den up from the cellar.
  • Nessa Ruhl surrenders and is carried off in cuffs. Pell Marrow's family is reunited; Gabe joins the party; 7 gp looted.

What changed

  • New: Nessa Ruhl, Alma Marrow, Gabe; Denn Marrow.
  • Belaskro Tich deputized as a constable by Malachai Wrent.
  • Princess Timbit formally joins the team.
  • Arcane Marshals ordered by the King's Council off the sabotage case.
  • Nessa Ruhl captured; four Pyreborn guards dead; the street convert dead.
  • Alma Marrow and Den freed (were held at The Drover's Rest).
  • Gabe joins the party.
  • Thread resolved: Pell Marrow's family. Advanced: The Kilnworks fire inquiry (every Marshal on it).

Clues

  • ⚑ Dray has GUS's letter but cannot act on anything touching the noble houses: partly landed (the Council's order came through Wrent instead).
  • ⚑ Wrent ordered off the rescue to the fire: landed.
  • ⚑ Wrent names Belaskro a contractor with authority, GUS attached: landed.
  • Other families held at the Drover's Rest: not landed.

Plans

  • Station and Drover's Rest played. The gala was not reached; the session ended with the party heading back with prisoners and the gala that evening.
