The sealed box is an arcane anchor meant to pull Barichello; the pull refracted and caught Gabriele

Known by
Dreeshen Vale

The truth

  • The box is an arcane anchor: a black stone glowing from its centre, supplied to Dreeshen Vale by the Dark Mage and activated with blood magic, meant solely to pull Barichello across realms. prep
  • The pull refracted at the barrier like light through a prism and produced a second anchor that caught Gabriele about three months later. prep
  • Conflict for Tom: Gabriele's page says his presence was never part of anyone's plan. Campaign/World Direction says he is "not here by accident": a cosmological counterweight, the one person who knows Barichello and can intervene. Both may hold (unplanned by Dreeshen, meant by the cosmos); Tom to rule.

Who knows

  • Dreeshen knows he had Barichello summoned; he knows nothing about Gabriele. prep
