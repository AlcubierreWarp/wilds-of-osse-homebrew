DM only

Secret boss of the Pyreborn; Bram Kell runs it day to day. The gang is an instrument and he will cut it loose the moment it becomes a liability.

Cold war with Queen Sabine: she targeted Cassian, he suspects the Crown, she knows the Pyreborn are his. Neither can move openly. They mirror each other: parents acting for children who do not know, each defending an idea.

Willing to hold Pell Marrow's wife and son for a month of forced labour.

The brand is the old Aerrowyn clan mark. Reveal order: Corvane first, Cassian one layer behind as the oblivious beneficiary, the Crown deepest.
