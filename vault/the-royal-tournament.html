The Royal Tournament

also Royal Tournament, the tournament, succession tournament
Lore · customfirst seen S3●●●●●

Varenthal's magecraft tournament that puts the crown, and every house's seat, up for contest

What it is

  • Corvane won a tournament that ennobled his family and took them from Shadowvale to Althon's Gate. S3
  • The party was hired to investigate attacks on tournament competitors, a month out from the tournament. S4
  • The Queen confirmed counterfeit shield runes, postponed the tournament two months, banned live combat training, and sealed the Crown's rune reserves, Elias included. S11
  • The champions were attacked at the Aerrowyn gala on the Queen's toast; Garrick Moravel and Aurelian Aldric died. S12
  • The founders' rule: the crown belongs to whoever is strong enough to take it, every generation, any citizen. Prince Elias must win outright to keep it. prep
  • Seats and holdings renew on a five-year cycle, so the field is crowded with competitors who do not want the throne. prep
DM only

Sabine thinks the constitution is a suicide note against Draven II and is clearing the field for Elias. Corvane is a true believer in it and fights the Crown's consolidation. prep See Sabine's campaign against the contenders.
