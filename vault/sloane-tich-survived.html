Belaskro's twin Sloane lived through the spider attack and was raised by the Oss'kai K'Tinga

Known by
Sloane Tich, Oss'kai K'Tinga

The truth

  • Twenty years ago a giant spider poisoned Aldous and Sloane and dragged them to its lair. Belaskro retreated and has believed both dead since. prep
  • An Oss'kai K'Tinga hunting party pulled Sloane out, recognized her Oss'kai blood and her mother, healed her and adopted her into the vale. prep
  • Her mother was with her for years and convinced her that reaching out to her birth family would expose Oss'kai secrecy around their magic. The mother is now gone from her life. prep
  • Sloane believes her father and twin both died the day she was taken. Neither twin knows the other lives. prep

Who knows

  • Sloane, and the K'Tinga who took her in. prep

How it could come out

  • Reunion thread for the wilderness arc, once the party reaches Oss'kai territory.
  • The sun and moon rings: hers matches his.
  • A K'Tinga scout who knows her face sees her twin in Belaskro.
