DM only

Lady Vale is his mother. The party will likely read the Council link as Seranthis state sponsorship; it is the opposite.

Loved Rosalia Whitlock. His mother paid her to leave Seranthis and kept from him where she went, so he believes she is lost. That devastation drives him: he blames the nobility for losing her. He does not know she volunteers at the Poor-Quarter Mission in Varenthal. (Tom's ruling, 2026-11-18)

Delivered Barichello to the Benefactor, an intermediary-only contact in the barrens, for magical and material support. Does not know who or what the Benefactor is or why it wanted the priest.

Believes he is Chaotic Good; he is Neutral Evil. That gap is the tragedy.

Endings: fight him, find Rosalia, or expose him to his followers.

Works through Seranthis criminal and underclass networks, not for the Kingdom. A possible tie to Belaskro's Oss'kai heritage is flagged, not built.
