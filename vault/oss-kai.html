Faction · peopleactive S3first seen S3●●●●●

Indigenous people of Ossë, organized in confederacies; Belaskro's hidden heritage

What it is

  • Indigenous people of Ossë. S3
  • Seranthis took Oss'kai women as brides by force. S3
  • Belaskro and Noémie Vayne bonded over the Oss'kai at the gala. S11
  • Organized in confederacies: Dawnwalker, Stonechant, Tideborn. prep
  • Mage adepts and mage healers carry formidable lineage magic; trained to live off the land; charged by their god to cleanse the wilds of poisoned magic. prep
  • Varenthal chose partnership with them; Seranthis chose dominance. prep
DM only

Belaskro is half-Oss'kai through his mother and does not know it.

Noémie Vayne's Cleansing Flare is Oss'kai lineage magic; House Vayne carries the blood.
