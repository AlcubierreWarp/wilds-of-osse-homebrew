Marshal-built constructs are property and second-class, with a rank ladder GUS is built to climb

What it is

  • Ilna refused to talk in front of an "arcane creature"; Belaskro declared GUS a person. S5
  • Healing works on GUS as mending. S8, table ruling
  • Constructs are second-class: not slaves, not persons. The Marshal cape is proof of ownership as much as authority. prep
  • A construct rank hierarchy exists inside the Marshals; GUS is built to want to climb it. prep
  • GUS is the only unserialized construct in the Marshals, an administrative anomaly from the ruined plate. prep
  • Marshal Intelligence can disguise a construct for undercover work, time-limited. S6
DM only

Cumulative mistreatment of constructs is the slow fuse for GUS's freedom-fighter path. Vigil is a disguised intelligence construct. prep
