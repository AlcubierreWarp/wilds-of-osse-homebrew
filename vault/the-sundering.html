The Sundering

also the sundering, The Founding Schism, Exile from Aglarond
Lore · historyfirst seen S3●●●●●

The founding split, 250 years ago, between the exiled houses Varenthal and Seranthis over the Oss'kai

What it is

  • Seranthis is a rival kingdom born of a bitter split about 250 years ago, which took Oss'kai women by force as brides during the Sundering. S3, Wrent&#x27;s briefing
  • The founding houses Varenthal and Seranthis were vassals of Aglarond in Faerûn. When King Brindor Olósynne died, his son Althon Olósynne exiled both: officially over suspicion in Brindor's death, in truth because they stood too close to the throne. They crossed Faerûn and sailed to Ossë's western shore. prep: setting page
  • The alliance broke over one question: what to do about the Oss'kai. Varenthal chose partnership and distance; Seranthis chose dominance, a public friendship layered with harsh laws that pushed those of Oss'kai heritage into a serf underclass. prep: setting page
  • The border between the kingdoms is a line between ideas of power and belonging. Some, like Master Ilvarin Thorne, argue the kingdoms must reunite against the barrens. prep
