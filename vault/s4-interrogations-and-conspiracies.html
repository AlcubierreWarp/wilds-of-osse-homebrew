S4 - Interrogations and Conspiracies

Session

The party beats Dask's crew in the Cut, finds Ilna's letter signed DV, and Marshal-Captain Rhys hires them off the books to find the noble house behind the gangs

What happened

  • Fight in The Cut: Krill Nye flees over barrels; Gabriele d'Avidicci pins Dask Corrin with a tackle and a spectral Saint Peter on horseback; GUS blinds Ivo with acid; Belaskro Tich knocks Dask out.
  • Interrogation: Gabriele heals Ivo and GUS re-burns his eyes at Gabriele's prompting until he breaks.
  • Ivo: they took Father Barichello two weeks ago and handed him to a merchant caravan bound for Kingdom of Seranthis; payment is due with another caravan from the south.
  • Dask carries a letter from Ilna Vesk, initialled "DV" and sealed with a raised fist: the kidnapping is seed money for keeping the Poor-Quarter hungry and angry, blaming the noble houses through the Sundermark Brides song; Old Tallow Yard is the handoff point.
  • GUS's steel defender delivers Dask and Ivo to Yorwick Dray's outpost. The party sleeps at The Glowing Goat; GUS stands as accidental night security.
  • Next morning, at The Sanctuary, Elowen Rhys lays it out: the Royal Tournament is a month away, and the Pyreborn and The Unbowed are stoking coordinated unrest.
  • Rhys believes a Varenthal noble house directs the Pyreborn and has ties to Seranthis; the houses block his requests.
  • Rhys hires the party off the books: 25 gp now, 30 more for actionable intelligence. He refuses Gabriele's request for classified files.
  • Malachai Wrent is told to "educate" GUS on discretion.

What changed

  • New: Elowen Rhys, The Unbowed (named), Krill Nye; Ilna Vesk known by name only.
  • Dask Corrin and Ivo captured, in Marshal custody. Krill Nye escaped, wounded.
  • The party holds Ilna's letter to Dask.
  • The party hired by Elowen Rhys and the Arcane Marshals, off the books.
  • Threads opened: Who is DV, The noble house behind the unrest, The tournament sabotage. Advanced: Find Father Barichello (caravan to Seranthis, two weeks gone).

Clues

  • ⚑ The caravan is two weeks gone and out of reach: landed (Ivo).
  • ⚑ The way forward runs through Ilna Vesk toward "DV": landed (the letter).
  • ⚑ Barichello was payment, traded to an unknown Benefactor: partly landed ("seed money"; the Benefactor not named).
  • ⚑ A second, incoming caravan will form up at the Old Tallow Yard: landed as overheard in S3 and named in the letter; not followed.
  • Raised fist seal of the conspirators: landed.
  • A noble house directs the Pyreborn, with Seranthis ties: landed (Rhys).

Plans

  • The Cut fight and the Briefing played; the Old Tallow Yard and Beneath the Saltings branches were skipped. The party never went to the yard.

Open going into S5

  • Who is "DV", and who is Ilna?
  • Which noble house is behind the Pyreborn and the Unbowed?
  • What will happen at the Royal Tournament?
  • The incoming payment caravan, still unwatched.
