Vigil is the first Steven

Plandraftdraft

Vigil is the original Steven and head of Marshal Intelligence, its M; every Steven unit was copied from Vigil

Thread
GUS's secret file

Idea

  • Vigil is the first Steven: the original construct every Marshal Intelligence spy unit was copied from, and the head of the service. Its M.
  • Why Vigil has not been back: the chief does not run agents in the field. Contact goes through the copies, which is the Steven running gag.
  • Why Vigil came to the gala: GUS's file was sealed to Vigil's own key, so only the original could open it. The head of the service personally took the Honey Cruller report, so GUS and Cruller matter more than the party knows.
  • Mirror for GUS: many Stevens, one Gus. Vigil was once the only one too, before the copies.
  • Wrent reported to Vigil, so Handler Signal Lost reached the top. Vigil now has a personal stake in GUS.
  • Vigil is rebuilt from scrap. The name came off a scrapped serial. Has real personality, for a bot.
  • Green ink: Vigil starts writing handwritten notes in green ink straight to GUS, on the chapters and lessons. Ease in: mysterious at first, plain standard writing, unsigned. Later, human humour about construct things. Rules in the repo's gus-intel-docs skill.
  • Options:
    • Serial numbers: Vigil's name came from a scrapped serial and GUS's serial was destroyed. Could be why Vigil took an interest in GUS.
    • The green ink is the tell only the original uses, so a player could someday work out which Steven was at the gala.
    • The annexes in GUS's file were written by "another construct called Steven". That could be Vigil, which puts rule 1.2's joke on the chief.
  • Open: did Vigil choose to be copied (a spymaster who built an army of themself) or was Vigil copied (something taken; a sympathetic mirror, late ally or warning)?

Requires

  • check: play has not shown anyone else heading Marshal Intelligence
