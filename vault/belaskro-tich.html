Who they are

  • Frontier bounty hunter from Shadowvale. S1
  • Grizzled, 40s. Rugged cowboy look: leather and plaid over armour, crossbow strapped to the leg, axe. Sharp features; something about him is slightly off. prep
  • Charming in shallow talk, useless in deep talk. Guarded; wants connection and has no idea how to look for it. prep
  • Protective and control-oriented. Spirals when things go bad, takes over and changes the plan mid-action without asking anyone. prep
  • Calls the people of Shadowvale "his people"; has a shack there. They mostly resent his drinking at the tavern. prep
  • Neutral between Varenthal and Seranthis: whoever pays. Hates mage-turned beasts. Tracking, survival, rescue, retrieval. prep
  • At twelve he watched Corvane and Maribel leave Shadowvale; Maribel gave him and Sloane the sun and moon rings. Has never sought out his uncle. S3
  • About twenty years ago a giant spider took his father and twin sister; he nearly died trying to get them back and believes both dead. prep
  • Goals: find why mage-turned beasts are increasing near the border towns and end it if the cause is concrete; find his mother and his heritage. Justin plans the Paladin multiclass once the character has grown into it. prep
  • Fear: letting anyone close, in case it gets them killed. Temptation not yet set. prep
DM only
  • Half-Oss'kai through his mother, and does not know it. His sharp features are the tell. Late-game discovery; the same thread as his goal of finding her. prep
  • His Oss'kai mother left the infant twins on their father's doorstep after a brief relationship. Unnamed and unbuilt; may have other children. prep
  • Sloane survived the spider. An Oss'kai K'Tinga hunting party pulled her from the lair, recognised her blood and her mother, healed her and took her into the vale. Her mother kept her from contacting her birth family. Neither twin knows the other lives. Reunion belongs to the wilderness arc. prep
  • The crescent moon in a sun is Corvane's old family mark from before House Aerrowyn. Bram Kell wears it because he works for Corvane. It is the one thread tying the Pyreborn to Corvane and Cassian, kept apart from the Barichello, Seranthis and Dark Mage chain. prep
  • In S7 the dying worker named "the Marquis" as the Pyreborn's leader, and only Belaskro heard it. The other players do not know. S7, Tom&#x27;s ruling
  • A past love in Shadowvale he avoids, sending companions on errands so he does not cross her. Not yet named. prep

S13Held the assassin from Cassian's bed until Corvane killed him; found the royal oathmark. Named Corvane's nephew in public. Took the guest suite.
