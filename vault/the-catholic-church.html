The Catholic Church

also Catholicism, the Church, the Pope, Rome
Lore · religionfirst seen S1●●●●●

Gabriele's Earth faith, made real in Ossë: the Pope, Latin, the saints and priestly power

What it is

  • Gabriele was carrying the sealed box for the Pope when the Santa Marie went down; praying over his cellmate, he cast guidance without knowing how. S1
  • Barichello's annotated prayer book shows his faith's words doing real magic here. S2, S3
  • Gabriele's spiritual weapon is Saint Peter on horseback. S4
  • His first convert in Ossë died in the Drover's Rest fight; Nessa told him the dead can be raised. S9
  • Catholicism, Jesus, the Pope and Latin are canon in Ossë by Tom's ruling before play. prep
DM only

Barichello's search for God through forbidden texts reached the Devil instead: the Big Bad. prep See Barichello is the anchor.
