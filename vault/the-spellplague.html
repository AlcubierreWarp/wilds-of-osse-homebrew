The Spellplague

also Spellplague, Spellplague of 1385 DR, change circles, change field, Spellplague flare, mage-turned beasts
Lore · historyprep●●●●●

The arcane catastrophe of 1385 DR whose aftershocks still warp Ossë's creatures and land

What it is

  • Since the Spellplague of 1385 DR, arcane aftershocks have warped animals, landscapes and ecosystems across Ossë, worst inside the change circles. Known roads are thin lines through a living wilderness. prep: setting page
  • Flares push mage-twisted creatures out of the barrens, usually in winter. Mage-turned beasts are escalating near the border towns. prep
  • No session summary records the word at the table yet; the party has met its effects as rumour and beasts. prep
DM only

The Big Bad was an Oss'kai Mage Adept caught in a Spellplague change field and fused with the shadow creatures she hunted. prep See The Benefactor's identity.

Open tasks on Tom's page: how magic works in the magical wastes, examples of wilds threats. prep
