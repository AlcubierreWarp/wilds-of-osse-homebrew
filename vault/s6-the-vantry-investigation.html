S6 - The Vantry Investigation

Session

A rune explosion at the Silverglass Annex leads to stolen Vantry blanks, a vanishing gnome supervisor, and Gabriele setting Vantry Runeworks alight as a diversion

What happened

  • Malachai Wrent's debrief is cut short: another shield rune has exploded at the Silverglass Annex. Exploding shield runes are now the main weapon against tournament competitors.
  • At the Annex, GUS finds six broken tablets: five stamped Kilnworks R Us, one stamped Vantry Runeworks that also carries illusion magic.
  • Janitor Ellard Fenn tells Belaskro Tich the shield let a bird through, then folded inward in a flash, burning two people and leaving the grounds untouched.
  • Wrent finds a receipt in coach Ander Voss's locker for five runes, none from Vantry.
  • Ovett Kallisch holds the Annex site.
  • At Vantry Runeworks in The Kilnworks, Gabriele d'Avidicci collides with a floating pallet and scatters blanks; he and Belaskro retreat to a tavern.
  • GUS, coached by Wrent, gets Guildmaster Ordell Vantry to talk: about a tenth of his blank tablets have vanished over two months with no break-ins. The Annex rune is his blank, never finished in his shop.
  • The gnome floor supervisor (Pell Marrow, unnamed at the table), overheard muttering about "one more month" and his family, walks into a wall and vanishes.
  • Over GUS's warning that a death would be a capital offence, Gabriele throws a torch through an office window as a diversion.
  • Wrent picks up a blank rune by the wall: "This has now just become interesting."

What changed

  • New: Ordell Vantry, Ander Voss (burned, offstage), Ellard Fenn, Pell Marrow (as "the gnome supervisor"), Silverglass Annex, Vantry Runeworks.
  • Vantry Runeworks set on fire by Gabriele.
  • GUS holds the six Annex fragments; Belaskro has blanks from the floor; Wrent has the Voss receipt.
  • Thread advanced: The tournament sabotage (now a hunt for the rune source).

Clues

  • ⚑ The Vantry maker's stamp on the fragments leads to the Runeworks: landed.
  • Illusion magic in the Vantry rune: landed.
  • Five runes bought, six found: landed.
  • Blank stock theft with no break-ins: landed.
  • The gnome's "one more month" and his family: landed.
  • Vantry is clean and eating the losses: partly landed (he cooperated; his hiding the losses from the guild did not come up).

Plans

  • Debrief, Annex and shop floor played. Ellard Fenn became the Annex janitor at the table instead of the Vantry inside man. The Under-Storage scene slid to S7, and the party's diversion fire was not in the plan.

Open going into S7

  • The factory is burning; what is behind the illusion wall?
  • Where did the gnome go, and who holds his family?
  • Are workers going to die in Gabriele's fire?
