The sealed box

also the box, the magical box, the papal chest, sealed chest, arcane anchor
Thing · artifactintact S1first seen S1●●●●●

Locked box recovered with Gabriele from the Santa Marie; radiates enough power to level Althon's Gate

Now

  • Recovered with Gabriele from the wreck; the Marshals cannot identify it. S1
  • Where it is held now is not recorded in the summaries. S1

What it is

  • A sealed chest the Pope ordered never opened; the Santa Marie carried it under Fra Alonso, who hanged a crewman for trying. prep
  • Wrent: it radiates enough magical power to level Althon's Gate, and a matching signature sits in Seranthis. S1
  • Quest line "Uncover the purpose of the magical box": no progress since. S12
DM only

It is an arcane anchor: a black stone glowing from its centre, supplied to Dreeshen Vale by the Dark Mage and activated with blood magic, meant solely to pull Barichello across. The pull refracted and caught Gabriele too. See Why Gabriele came to Ossë. prep
