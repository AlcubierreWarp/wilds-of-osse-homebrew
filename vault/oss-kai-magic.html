The hidden spellcraft of the Oss'kai: Mage Adept and Mage Healer lineages, and a god-given mission to cleanse the wilds

What it is

  • Outsiders see the Oss'kai only in fragments: limited trade, rumour, and in Seranthis intermarriage and coerced assimilation. prep
DM only
  • The Oss'kai are far from powerless. Mage Adepts and Mage Healers carry lineages of formidable spellcraft; children train young to live off the land, move quietly and defend themselves. prep
  • Their god handed them a mission: clean the wildlands of the poisoned magical influence of the Spellplague. The Oss'kai K'Tinga carry it out. prep
  • Strong users' hair bleaches. Sharp features are a quiet tell of Oss'kai blood (Belaskro, Rosalia, Noémie). prep
  • Lineage light shows as green-gold motes; Noémie Vayne passes hers off as sorcery (Cleansing Flare). prep
  • Confederacies named for the summary glossary: Dawnwalker, Stonechant, Tideborn. Pantheon and how the Oss'kai deal with their gods: still open. prep
  • Inspired by the Tayledras of the Heralds of Valdemar. prep, DM note
