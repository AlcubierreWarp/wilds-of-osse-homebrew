  • Holds the Council through the Aldric bloc, which closes ranks around her by reflex. prep
  • Needs: one month to pass quietly. prep

  • Catchphrase: "A crown that can be taken every generation is not a crown. It is a wager." prep
  • Three words: patient, certain, unreachable. prep
DM only

She is the architect of the campaign removing strong tournament contenders. Cassian's Silverglass "accident" was an early case. The King and Elias know nothing.

Runs a standing intelligence network; that is how she reached Cassian inside a Marshal-supervised facility.

Knows the Pyreborn answer to Corvane and cannot move on him without convicting herself. A cold war; she and Corvane mirror each other.

She is winning: Elias is favourite, the Council is friendly, Cassian is off the board, and the party burned the Pyreborn's rune supply for her.

Elias is the arc's most important innocent. The party may decide what he learns about his mother.
