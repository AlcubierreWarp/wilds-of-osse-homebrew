The sun-and-moon brand

also crescent moon inside a sun, Pyreborn crest, sun and moon crest
Lore · termfirst seen S2●●●●●

A crescent moon inside a sun: the Pyreborn crest, and the mark on Corvane's wagon when he left Shadowvale

What it is

  • Bram Kell wore it on his chest at the mission siege; Belaskro knew it from his uncle's wagon at twelve. S2
  • In Belaskro's flashback, Maribel said it stood for the bond of two families, Corvane's sun and her moon. S3
  • The Pyreborn signet ring Timbit took bears it. S7
  • Nessa Ruhl's ledger pages are headed with it. S10
DM only

It is Corvane's old personal brand from before House Aerrowyn, mixing the Tich sun and the Sorne moon; Maribel asked for the moon. Aldous worried it would raise questions one day. Bram does not know what it means. It is the single thread from the Pyreborn to Corvane, and on to Cassian. prep
