The champions' assassins

also The Knives, Protect the Tournament Champions, Protect Lord Cassian Aerrowyn
Threadopenfirst seen S11

The question

  • Who sent the Knives, who holds the contract on Cassian, and why did they wait for the Queen's toast? S11

What the party knows

  • At the Queen's toast "to the champions", the balcony musicians teleported into the crowd with daggers. S11
  • The blades are poisoned; a nick fells a champion. Garrick Moravel, Aurelian Aldric and Malachai Wrent died; Éclair Exquisite lies unconscious; Noémie Vayne is down. S12
  • The killers dimension-door out once their mark is dead. S12
  • House Dreveth's table had no assassin; Gabriele d'Avidicci reads it as someone staging the blame on Seranthis. S12
  • A royal page's shield bubble trapped Corvane on the dais. S12
  • A bell tolled each round as another ward around Cassian's room failed. S12
  • Upstairs, a blue-robed assassin told Belaskro he is "not on the contract": Cassian is the target. S12
  • The assassin in Cassian's room bore a royal oathmark; Corvane killed him. S13
  • Grey handed over the guard order under Corvane's seal and fled. S13

Clues

  • The toast as signal. S11
  • No assassin at the Dreveth table. S12
  • "You're not on the contract." S12
  • Lesser restoration clears the poison. S12
  • Royal oathmark behind the assassin's right ear. S13
  • Dunmore's testimony and the staff list signed by order of Grey. S13
