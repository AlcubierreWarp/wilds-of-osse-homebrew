The question

  • Where is Father Barichello, who took him, and why? S1

What the party knows

  • Barichello helped at the Poor-Quarter Mission until he vanished two weeks before the party arrived. S1
  • Men with Seranthis accents dragged him toward the docks. S3
  • Dask Corrin's crew handed him to a merchant caravan bound for Kingdom of Seranthis; it is two weeks gone. S4
  • Ilna Vesk traded him to Dreeshen Vale's people for money and equipment for The Unbowed; she does not know where in Seranthis he went. S5
  • The Vale name ties to Lady Vale of the Seranthis King's Council. S5
  • Gabriele d'Avidicci wears Barichello's rosary as a promise to return it. S3
  • Jared Dreveth and his lady showed no recognition of the rosary: House Dreveth's leaders seem uninvolved. S11

Clues

  • Lux Aveline Corstane recalls a "Christian" man who vanished two weeks ago. S1
  • Barichello's prayer book, its annotations working spells. S2; spells understood S3
  • His rosary on the mission altar. S2
  • Rosalia Whitlock's eyewitness account of the kidnapping. S3
  • Ivo's confession: the caravan south. S4
  • Ilna's letter to Dask: the kidnapping as "seed money". S4
  • Ilna's confession and the Lady Vale link. S5
  • Dreveth non-reaction to the rosary. S11
