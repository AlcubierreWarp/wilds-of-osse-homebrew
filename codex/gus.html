Session Log

SessionUpdates
S1Hauled into the Sanctuary as salvage by Belaskro. Five Marshal mages repaired him; Wrent named him Gus and recruited him.
S2Leapt through a window to douse the kindling with acid. Fought in the street with his steel defender. Logged a rosary carrying an unknown magic.
S3Dray had his steel defender sent to the docks. Ordered by Wrent to watch Gabriele; read it so literally that Gabriele was cuffed and fined. The rosary passed to Gabriele.
S4Shocking grasp on Dask; acid in Ivo's eyes, twice, the second at Gabriele's prompt. His defender hauled both prisoners to the Marshals. Powered down as the Goat's accidental night guard.
S5Took Wrent's caution as a hint at undercover work. Arrested Krill Nye and the proprietor, then freed Krill to lead them to his boss. Tied Dreeshen Vale to Lady Vale by records.
S6Found six broken runes at the Annex: five Kilnworks R Us, one Vantry with illusion magic. Talked Ordell Vantry into an interview. Warned that a death in the fire would be capital, then went along.
S7Dropped unconscious on the stairs by a mace crit; stabilised by Timbit.
S8Wrote to Marshal dispatch about the safe house. Chased down and cuffed Gabriele. Mended by Kallisch.
S9Roped Gabriele to his defender. Wrent named Belaskro his conscience. His defender crushed two thugs. Arrested Nessa Ruhl.
S10Recharged at Dray's outpost; had a cape altered at Tip's on an IOU. Admitted to the gala.
S11"Steven" of Marshal Intelligence unlocked an encrypted file in his memory. Reported Honey Cruller's remarks on foreign interest. Met Noémie Vayne.
S12Shocked a prone Knife and sent for his steel defender, which struck the Vayne Knife. Heard "Handler Vitals: Disconnected" as Wrent died.
S13Heard "Handler signal lost" and followed EP-07; gives his name as Steven. Questioned Captain Dunmore and ordered roadblocks for Grey. Answered a stranger's contact phrase by the manor doors.
