Lore 9

  • The Royal TournamentCorvane won a tournament that ennobled his family and took them from Shadowvale to Althon's Gate.
  • Shield runesWrent briefed that exploding shield runes are the mechanism behind the tournament attacks.
  • The SunderingSeranthis is a rival kingdom born of a bitter split about 250 years ago, which took Oss'kai women by force as brides during the Sundering.
  • The sun-and-moon brandBram Kell wore it on his chest at the mission siege; Belaskro knew it from his uncle's wagon at twelve.
  • Constructs in VarenthalIlna refused to talk in front of an "arcane creature"; Belaskro declared GUS a person.
  • The Ballad of the Sundermark BridesAt the Glowing Goat the bartender told the party its dark history; Wrent framed it as Seranthis taking Oss'kai women by force as brides during the Sundering.
  • The Catholic ChurchGabriele was carrying the sealed box for the Pope when the Santa Marie went down; praying over his cellmate, he cast guidance without knowing how.
  • KissedA mere nick from a Knife's blade dropped Éclair, Garrick and Aurelian in the first moments of the gala attack.
  • Tolling the DeadGabriele loosed it during the mission siege without knowing what it was.
