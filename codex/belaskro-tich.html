Session Log

SessionUpdates
S1Hauled scrapped GUS into the Sanctuary and argued reclamation rate with Kallisch. Wrent paid, ordered the repair, and hired him with a handshake that carried a magical test.
S2From the mission window, recognised the crescent moon in a sun on Bram's chest: his uncle's wagon crest. Covered the escape with fog cloud.
S3Shadowvale flashback: Maribel's rings, Corvane leaving ennobled, Maribel later dying bearing Cassian. Overheard the Seranthis crew at the Goat, marked Dask, paid Gabriele's fifteen-silver fine, and got two gold added to his contract.
S4Knocked Dask out in the dockyard alley. Present for Ivo's interrogation and the letter naming Ilna and "DV". Took Marshal-Captain Rhys's off-books contract.
S5Blew the Tallow Yard approach. Spotted the lip-sync at the Fair Maiden. Refused to let Ilna send GUS out and declared him a person.
S6Drew Ellard Fenn's clean account of the Annex attack. Pocketed blank rune tablets at the Runeworks.
S7Whipped down a fleeing worker who, dying, said Belaskro looked just like "him", the leader of the Pyreborn. Did not see the signet ring.
S8Corvane warned him off the investigation, offered him work, and invited him to dinner to meet Cassian.
S9Deputized as a constable and named GUS's conscience. Invited to Corvane's gala. Took a coin from Gabriele. His falling log pile blew the raid; freed Gabe's chain and carried Denn out.
S10Dressed by Tip. Met Cassian. Corvane told him of Maribel's death and the Council's "quality control" line.
S11Bonded with Noémie Vayne over frontier hunting and the Oss'kai; left her table tipsy. Learned from Dray that brigands freed Nessa.
S12Ran upstairs on Corvane's shout; one ward crystal of six still lit. Body-checked the blue-robed assassin off the bed and marked him; told "You're not on the contract." One of his blanks broke the royal shield.
S13Held the assassin from Cassian's bed until Corvane killed him; found the royal oathmark. Named Corvane's nephew in public. Took the guest suite.
