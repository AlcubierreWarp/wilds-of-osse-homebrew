Session Log

SessionUpdates
S1Wrecked off the Santa Marie; woke in a Sanctuary cell. Cleared by Wrent's truth device and taken along on the Barichello lead. Cast guidance unknowingly while praying over Corwin Thatch.
S2Searched the burning mission and found Barichello's annotated prayer book. Escaped through Lux Corstane's tunnel.
S3Confirmed the rosary was Barichello's and put it on as a promise. Matched the marginalia to real spells, including "Tolling the Dead". Cuffed and fined when GUS read his orders literally.
S4Summoned St. Peter on horseback and pinned Dask with a natural 20. Healed Ivo so GUS could burn him again. Refused classified files in place of pay.
S5Worked Dask with Wrent and drew out Ilna's and Dreeshen Vale's names. Staged an outburst so Dask could be released and tailed. Put Ilna down with a guiding bolt. Healed and paid Dask: his first informant.
S6Crashed into a floating pallet at the Runeworks and alerted the floor. Overheard the gnome supervisor. Started the diversion fire through an office window.
S7Announced the inspection and set off the burn order. Smothered a burning machine with his body. His fire took the Runeworks and spread into the Kilnworks.
S8Tried to rescue Pell's family alone; GUS chased him down and cuffed him.
S9Uncuffed when Timbit claimed him as bodyguard and priest. Preached a street sermon to cover the raid. His first convert joined the fight and was killed by Nessa, who told him the dead can be raised.
S10Bought formal clerical robes from Tip by intimidation and was banned from the shop. Talked GUS into the gala.
S11Dropped the rosary before the Dreveth lord and lady: no recognition (Insight 19). Talked faith and power with Jared Dreveth.
S12Pulled Jared under a table and set St. Peter on the Vayne assassin. Read the untouched Seranthis table as a frame. Threw the blank rune to Timbit rather than help Wrent; Wrent died.
S13Revived Wrent with revivify; Barichello's cross burned a mark into his palm. Gave rites over Garrick and Aurelian. Freed from supervision. Jared Dreveth invited him to Seranthis; he declined for now. Went to the Meow embassy as Timbit's bodyguard.
