What the party knows

  • It was recovered with him after the wreck. Marshal mages read enough power in it to level Althon's Gate. S1
  • A matching magical signature has been detected in Kingdom of Seranthis. S1
  • The Arcane Marshals hold it at The Sanctuary. S1

Clues

  • Matching Seranthis signature, from Marshal intelligence. S1
  • Found near Gabriele, not on him. S1
