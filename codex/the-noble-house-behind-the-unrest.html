The question

  • Which Varenthal noble house is directing the Pyreborn and the unrest, and how is it tied to Seranthis? S4

What the party knows

  • Elowen Rhys hired the party off the books: 25 gp paid, 30 gp more on actionable intelligence. S4
  • It is black money: if caught, the Arcane Marshals disavow them. S5
  • The houses block Rhys's requests; the King's Council denies every Pyreborn raid. S4, S5
  • Seranthis nobility is tied in through Dreeshen Vale and Lady Vale. S5
  • Corvane Aerrowyn knows of the investigation, warned Belaskro off, and stared at the Queen with open hatred. S8
  • The Council pulled every Marshal onto the Kilnworks fire; Belaskro was deputized to carry on. S9
  • Corvane sits on the King's Council, which calls the rune failures quality control. S10
  • Honey Cruller: two factions are forming, Corvane's pro-tournament camp and the Crown loyalists. S11

Clues
